The "Golden Silk" Turkey Cottage Pie

Zapiekanka Pasterska — High-Protein Artisan Edition

Traditional beef cottage pie delivers heavy saturated fats and turns into a watery sponge when frozen. This high-volume formula pairs 1,200 g of lean turkey breast mince with a rich bone-broth ragù under an anti-syneresis potato-carrot silk mash and blistered gratin crust. Engineered for large-batch freezing, effortless reheating, and gentle digestion.

📐 Pan Geometry & Batch Sizing

This batch yields approximately ~4.2 kg raw preparation, settling to ~3.8 kg cooked weight (6 generous portions of ~630–700g each).

Baking Pan Options:
• Single Large Casserole: 35 cm × 25 cm deep ceramic or roasting dish (depth ≥ 6 cm).
• Two Medium Bakes: Split evenly between two 25 cm × 20 cm dishes (bake one fresh for 3 days, freeze the second unbaked).
• 6 Individual Meal-Prep Tins: Portion into 6 oven- and freezer-safe glass or foil meal-prep containers (~750–800 ml each).

🔥 Step-by-Step Method

  1. Steam the Golden Silk Mash
    Place the 800g peeled potato chunks and 670g peeled carrot chunks in a large steamer basket. Steam over boiling water for 18–20 minutes until a paring knife glides through the carrot core with zero resistance.
    Drain thoroughly and let rest in the hot pot off-heat for 2 minutes to let surface moisture flash-steam off.
    Add 20g ghee, 135ml warm milk, 3/4 tsp salt, and nutmeg. Mash thoroughly, then whip vigorously with a wooden spoon until glossy and completely smooth.
    💡 Visual Cue: Should hold soft peaks like warm buttercream.
  2. Simmer the Turkey & Root Base
    Warm 13g ghee or oil in a wide heavy pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Sauté the 470g diced carrots and 270g diced parsnips for 5 minutes until aromatic.
    Add the 1,200g lean turkey mince. Break it apart gently with a wooden spatula for 3–4 minutes just until opaque. Avoid aggressive browning to keep lean poultry tender.
    Whisk 33g bone broth powder into 530ml warm stock/water. Pour into the pot along with 530g passata, 27ml Worcestershire sauce, thyme, salt, and black pepper.
    Cover and simmer gently on low heat for 15 minutes until root vegetables are tender. Stir in the potato starch slurry (13g starch + 35ml cold water) and simmer for 60 seconds until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the mince.
  3. Assemble & Texture the Crust
    Pour the hot turkey base into your baking dish (approx. 3.5 cm depth).
    Spoon the warm mash on top. Gently spread with a spatula, sealing completely against the outer edges to trap steam.
    Drag fork tines across the top in a cross-hatch pattern to create texture peaks. Scatter the 120g of grated mature cheddar evenly across the surface.
  4. Bake & Crisp
    Bake at 190°C Fan (200°C Conventional / 400°F) for 20–22 minutes until bubbling vigorously at the edges.
    Switch oven to Broil / Fan Grill at 220°C (430°F) for 3–5 minutes until the cheese ridges blister into dark golden spots.
    🛑 Resting Gate: Rest on the counter for 10 minutes before slicing. The starch matrix sets as it cools, giving clean, intact portions that lift out cleanly.

🧪 Food Science & Digestive Safeguards

🌿 Sensitive Gut & IBD Safeguards
• Zero Insoluble Abrasives: All carrots and potatoes are completely peeled and steamed until cellular pectins break down; zero coarse skins, seeds, or raw cruciferous fibers to irritate delicate gut mucosa.
• High-Density Recovery: Delivers 64g of lean, bioavailable protein per portion.
• Gut Barrier Support: Bone broth powder supplies concentrated glycine and proline peptides.
• Ultra-Low Lactose: Naturally aged mature cheddar ferments to <0.1% lactose.
❄️ The Anti-Syneresis Freezing Fix
Standard potato mash turns spongy and weeps water when frozen due to amylose starch retrogradation. Blending carrots into the potato introduces branched pectins that sterically disrupt amylose crystallization. Combined with emulsified ghee and milk, it thaws into a smooth, coherent cream without separating.
