📐 Pan Geometry & Physics

A 1,200g mince batch yields approx ~4.2 kg total raw preparation, settling to ~3.8 kg cooked weight.

Pan Options:
• Single Large Casserole: 35 cm × 25 cm deep ceramic/glass roaster (depth: ~6.0 cm).
• Two Standard Bakes: Split between two 25 cm × 20 cm dishes (eat one across 4 days, freeze the second whole unbaked).
• 8 Individual Tins: Portion into 8 oven/freezer-safe foil meal-prep containers (~500–550ml each).

🔥 Step-by-Step Culinary Protocol

  1. Steam the Golden Silk Mash
    Place the 800g peeled potato chunks and 670g peeled carrot chunks in a large steamer pot. Steam over boiling water for 18–20 minutes until a paring knife glides through the carrot core with zero resistance.
    Drain thoroughly and let rest in the hot pot off-heat for 2 minutes to let excess surface water flash-steam off.
    Add 20g ghee, 135ml warm milk, 3/4 tsp salt, and nutmeg. Mash thoroughly, then whip with a sturdy wooden spoon until velvety and glossy.
    💡 Visual Cue: Should hold soft peaks like warm buttercream.
  2. Simmer the Turkey & Root Base
    In a wide Dutch oven, warm 13g ghee over medium heat. Sauté the 470g diced carrots and 270g diced parsnips for 5 minutes until aromatic.
    Add the 1,200g of 2% turkey breast mince. Break it apart gently with a wooden spatula for 3–4 minutes just until opaque. Do not hard-sear to dryness.
    Whisk 33g beef bone broth powder into 530ml warm water/stock. Add to the pot with 530g passata, 27ml Worcestershire sauce, thyme, salt, and black pepper.
    Cover and simmer gently on low heat for 14–16 minutes until root cubes are tender. Stir in the potato starch slurry (13g starch + 35ml cold water). Simmer for 60 seconds until the gravy turns into a lustrous glaze that clings to the mince.
  3. Assembly & Gratin Ridges
    Pour the piping hot turkey base into your baking dish (layer depth: ~3.5 cm).
    Spoon the warm Golden Silk Mash on top. Gently smooth with an offset spatula or back of a spoon, sealing tightly against all outer edges to lock in steam.
    Drag fork tines across the mash in a criss-cross pattern to create high ridges. Scatter the 120g of finely grated mature cheddar evenly over top.
  4. The Thermal Bake & Maillard Blister
    Bake at 190°C Fan (200°C Conventional) for 20–22 minutes. The hot base will begin bubbling vigorously at the dish perimeter.
    Switch oven to Fan Grill at 220°C for 3–5 minutes. Watch closely through the glass door as the cheese ridges bubble and blister into dark amber spots.
    🛑 Resting Gate: Rest on counter for 10 minutes before cutting. The starch matrix sets on cooling, giving razor-clean square cuts that lift out without collapsing.

🧪 Food Science & Clinical Safeguards

🌿 Ulcerative Colitis (UC) Safeguards (Jola)
• Zero Insoluble Abrasives: All carrots and potatoes are completely peeled and steamed until cellular pectins break down; zero coarse skins, seeds, or raw cruciferous fibers to abrade colonic mucosa.
• Corticosteroid Catabolism Counter: Delivers 48g of bioavailable protein per serving.
• Tight-Junction Repair: Grass-fed beef bone broth powder supplies abundant glycine and proline to support gut barrier repair.
• Low Lactose: Mature British cheddar naturally ferments to <0.1% lactose.
❄️ The Anti-Syneresis Freezing Fix
Standard potato mash turns into a watery, spongy mess in the freezer due to amylose retrogradation crystallization. Blending 50% sweet carrot into the potato introduces branched pectins that sterically obstruct amylose crystallization. Combined with emulsified ghee and milk, it freezes and thaws as a velvety, coherent cream without weeping water.
